# Brand brief: Product name

_Approved by the user before the brand book is written. Plain words, real values. Replace every italic line with what was decided._

## Product and people

_What the product is in one sentence, who uses it, how often, and the moment they open it._

## Feel

_The three feel words, which one wins when they collide, and one line on what each means for this product._

## References and what was taken

_Each reference by name, with what we take from it and what we leave. Words only, no files._

## Color

_Each role (canvas, surface, text, primary, accent, success, warning, danger) with its hex in light and in dark, and what primary and accent are for._

## Type

_The display, body and mono faces, each with its fallback stack and license, the display weight and tracking, and the base size._

## Shape and density

_The radii in px (small, medium, large), the border weight, the spacing feel (roomy or dense), and how much a screen holds._

## Motion

_How things move, or that they barely do: durations, easing, and what is never animated._

## Voice

_How the product speaks: three words, two example lines, and the words it never uses._

## Rejected, with reasons

_Every direction, color, face or idea that was turned down, each with the reason in one line._
