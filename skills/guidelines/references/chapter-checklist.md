# Chapter checklist

Answer every line yes or no before the brand book is shown. A no is fixed, not explained. The template's own copy is about a clinic calendar called Sample; any line that still reads like it is a no.

## 01 Foundations

- Does the product line say what this product is and who it is for, in two sentences?
- Are the three feel words this brand's words, from the brief, and not Calm, Exact, Warm?
- Does each principle name a real tie it decides, one this team will meet?
- Would a stranger tell this brand from another after reading only this chapter?

## 02 Mark

- Is the wordmark the product's name, spelled and cased as the brand writes it?
- Is the placeholder callout still there, unless the user gave a mark of their own?
- Are the four sizes, the three backgrounds and the clear space shown for the mark in use?
- Does the mark read on primary, using the primary text color?

## 03 Color

- Do the role lines say what primary and accent are for in this product, and what they are never for?
- Does every `color-*` token have a dark value that was chosen, not copied from light?
- Does the contrast table show Pass on every row, in light and in dark?
- Is the accent used for one job only, and is that job written down?

## 04 Typography

- Are the three faces named in the tokens the ones the brief chose, with a fallback for each?
- If `fonts` could not inline a face, does the chapter say the fallback is in use?
- Do the scale samples speak in this product's words?
- Does the reading paragraph state real rules for line length and numbers?

## 05 Spacing

- Do the space, radius, shadow and border lines describe this product's screens, not the template's?
- Do the radii match the feel words (sharp for exact, round for soft, and so on)?
- Is every shadow tied to something that floats?

## 06 Iconography

- Does the rule line give the stroke or fill, the grid and the corner style of the set this brand uses?
- Do the eight icons stand for actions this product has?
- Does every icon in the page sit beside a word or carry an accessible label?

## 07 Components

- Is there a usage line for every component, specific to this product?
- Are all fourteen components present, each with its states visible at rest, in light and in dark?
- Does every specimen speak in this product's words (names, actions, amounts), in the light instance and in the dark pocket?
- Do `data-tokens` on each light instance name the tokens the component really uses?

## 08 Motion

- Do the three demo lines say where each motion appears in this product?
- Do the timing tokens match the feel (quick for dense tools, slower for calm ones)?
- Does every animation stop with reduced motion?

## 09 Voice

- Do the three tone lines describe how this brand speaks, in its own words?
- Are the four do and don't pairs real strings this product would show?
- Is every don't a mistake this team could plausibly make?

## 10 Responsive

- Do the three breakpoint lines say what changes on this product's main screen at each width?
- Are the target size, visible focus and contrast rules kept as written?
- Was the page looked at in a narrow window, with nothing clipped and nothing scrolling sideways?

## Every chapter

- Does `check` report `0 problems`, and is every `unfilled:` line gone?
- Does the contrast table pass in both themes?
- Is every state of every component visible without hovering or clicking?
- Is the placeholder callout present, unless the user supplied a mark?
- Is the title `<Name> · Design guidelines`, with the product's name?
- Is there no em dash anywhere in the file?
- Is there no exclamation mark in any text?
- Is nothing copied from a reference: no name, color, phrase or layout taken from someone else's brand?
