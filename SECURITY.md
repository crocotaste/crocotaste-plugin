# Security

## Reporting a vulnerability

Please report it privately through GitHub: open the repository's **Security** tab and choose **Report a vulnerability**. Do not open a public issue for it.

Tell us what you found, how to reproduce it, and what it lets someone do. We reply in the same report, fix it on a branch, and credit you in the release notes if you want.

## Scope

The plugin runs on your machine. Its script reads and writes files in your repo and serves a read-only preview on localhost; it makes no network calls except fetching open fonts when you ask it to. Reports about the skills, the script, the templates or the examples are in scope.

Problems with the Crocotaste app itself go through the same form; we route them.
