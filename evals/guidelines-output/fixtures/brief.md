# Brand brief: Tidewater

Approved by the user.

## Product and people

Tidewater is a web app that shows the tides at a harbor and the best fishing windows around them. It is used by small-boat owners and weekend anglers, a few times a week, usually on a phone at the dock before they decide whether to go out.

## Feel

Calm, weathered, exact. Calm wins when they collide: a tide table is read in a hurry and in cold light, so nothing on screen shouts. Weathered means paper and rope, not shine. Exact means every number is where the eye expects it.

## References and what was taken

- A printed nautical almanac: the tables, the quiet two-color rhythm. We leave its density.
- A tide clock on a harbor wall: one big dial, one number. We leave the brass and the skeuomorphic gloss.

## Color

Light, then dark.

- Canvas: #f6f1e7 / #0e1a24
- Surface: #ece5d6 / #15252f
- Text: #14212b / #eef2f1
- Primary (the tide, buttons, links, the chart line): #0b4f6c / #6fc0dd
- Accent (the fishing window, used rarely and only to mark a good time): #e8590c / #ff8a4c
- Success: #2f7d4f / #62c58b
- Warning: #9a6a00 / #e2b541
- Danger: #b3261e / #ff8f86

Primary is for what moves with the water. Accent is for the one window worth going out in, never for decoration.

## Type

- Display: Newsreader, a serif, weight 600, tracking -0.01em, fallback Georgia, serif. Open license.
- Body: Inter, weights 400 and 500, fallback system-ui, sans-serif. Open license.
- Mono: JetBrains Mono, for times and heights, fallback ui-monospace, monospace. Open license.
- Base size 16 px.

## Shape and density

- Radii: small 4 px, medium 6 px, large 10 px.
- Border weight 1 px, in a muted sand or deep blue, never black.
- Spacing is roomy on the dial and tight in the tables. A screen holds one tide chart and one table.

## Motion

Barely any. Fades and the chart line drawing in, 180 ms to 240 ms, ease-out. Never animate numbers while they are being read, and never bounce.

## Voice

Plain, dry, helpful. Three words: steady, brief, seaworthy.

- "High water at 14:32. The window opens an hour before."
- "No good window today. The wind turns at noon."

It never says "awesome", "epic" or "catch the wave".

## Rejected, with reasons

- A bright turquoise primary: it reads as a holiday brochure, not a tool for the dock.
- A hand-drawn, wavy display face: charming, but hard to read in cold light on a small screen.
